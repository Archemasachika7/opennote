# OpenNote — "Notes that run." · Treatment

Style: **Living Screencast**. 1920×1080, 30 fps, ~78 s. English narrator (Kokoro, offline), burned-in caption pills + `.srt`. No storyboard stop (user's choice).

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **Feature list that turns.** Five chapter pills, one feature per line, the list ends by folding into the app icon. Clear, but it is the cliché of every walkthrough.
2. **A day of one student.** Morning lecture → afternoon problem set → night revision → next morning on the phone. The app's real light theme by day, its real dark "Midnight Studio" theme at night, and the music shifts with the hour. Every feature is a moment a student actually needs it.
3. **A race.** Paper notebook vs OpenNote side by side, the mascot winning on the right. Funny, but OpenNote's signature (live pages) has no paper equivalent, so the race stalls exactly where the film should peak.

**Chosen: 2, "a day of one student".** It is the user's own story (the handoff note: a student who keeps AI visualisers inside lecture notes), it gives a real arc (light → dark → morning on another device), and the theme switch is a native "film changes state" move this product genuinely has.

## 2. Logline and arc

A student's notebook spends one day being written, recorded, quizzed and carried across devices, and its little ink-drop sprite discovers the notebook can *run*.

- **Setup (0–20 s)** morning, light theme: an empty library, a new notebook, handwriting, a shape snap, a lasso move.
- **Turn (20–37 s)** an AI artifact is pasted into a Live page and starts running between ink pages; the sprite rides its slider. This is the peak.
- **Development (37–62 s)** afternoon lecture: recording, tap-a-stroke-to-seek, on-device transcript. Night (theme flips dark): tape, flashcards, whiteboard.
- **Ending (62–78 s)** sync is encrypted (spotlight freeze), the window divides into web / Android / Windows; the sprite falls asleep on the finished notebook card; diegetic title "Notes that run."

## 3. Benchmark

- **Apple "Guided Tour" product videos.** Learn: calm one-feature-per-sentence narration, the real UI as the only set, long enough holds. Don't take: their UI, voice, music, any shot.
- **Screen Studio-style recordings.** Learn: auto-zoom to the cursor, spring cursor, click push-ins, a window floating on a wallpaper. Don't take: their wallpapers or branding.

## 4. Mascot

**"Blot"**, an original ink drop, 12×13 pixel grid, electric blue `#2F8CFF` (no OpenNote UI element uses this blue at that saturation; amber, mint, teal and red are taken; no purple anywhere, per the project's hard rule). White pixel eyes; poses: idle, crouch, jump, land, walk A/B, cheer, sleep. Props: pixel pencil, "!" and "z" glyphs, sparkles, dust.

Blot is the notebook's agency made visible: it is born from the pen tip's first stroke (match-cut origin), it stands on top edges of real UI (toolbar, page header, slider track, flashcard, card), never on text that must be read.

## 9. Subtitles and titles

- Caption pill: bottom centre, frosted dark glass (`rgba(18,19,24,.72)` + 1 px `#2A2D37`), Inter 30 px, white. Never over the element being discussed; Blot's lane is above the pill.
- Chapter pill: top left, "01 Write · 02 Live pages · 03 Lecture · 04 Revise · 05 Everywhere", Space Grotesk, the amber dot is the one accent.
- KeyCastr HUD: bottom right, JetBrains Mono keycaps ("Ctrl V", "Space", "3").
- Title is diegetic only: the notebook title "Physics 201" and, at the end, the library header "OpenNote" with "Notes that run." in the search box. No logo card.
