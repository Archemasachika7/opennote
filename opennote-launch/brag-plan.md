# Brag Plan: OpenNote

## What is this app?
A free, offline, no-account Goodnotes-style notebook (web, Android, Windows) where pasted AI artifacts (HTML, SVG, React) become live, runnable pages between your handwritten pages.

## The angle
"Notes that run." The one thing no paper notebook and no other note app does: a visualiser from your AI chat keeps running inside your lecture notes, and you can still write on it with a pen. Told as one student's day, so every feature shows up at the moment it's needed.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
An empty OpenNote library in the morning light theme; the cursor clicks **New notebook** and the first ink stroke pops out as a pixel ink-drop sprite. Line: "This is OpenNote. A free notebook where notes can run."

## Key moments
- Paste → **Save** → the "Harmonic Wave Superposition" Live page starts running between ink pages; the sprite drags its slider and the wave changes.
- Tap a handwritten stroke and the lecture recording jumps to that exact moment.
- Tape hides a term → **Generate flashcards from Tape** → a study card rated **Good**.

## Outro / punchline
"Web, Android and Windows. No charges. No ads." → the sprite falls asleep on the finished notebook card; the search box reads "Notes that run."

## User flow worth showing
New notebook → write (pen, shape snap, lasso) → insert **Live page**, paste, **Save** → it runs → record lecture, tap ink to seek → tape → flashcards → encrypted sync → the same notebook on web, Android, Windows.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: calm Apple-style guided tour with a playful pixel sidekick
- Interpretation: one feature per sentence, long enough holds, the humour comes only from the sprite's acting.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: ~78 s (user asked for a thorough walkthrough; overrides the 15–25 s default)

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#121318` (dark), `#f4f4f6` (light)
- Accent: `#F5A524` amber (light theme `#B45309`); Live pages `#3DDC97` mint; study `#4FD1C5`
- Text: `#E8E9EE`
- Display font: Space Grotesk · Body: Inter · Code: JetBrains Mono
- Strongest visual element: the mint-edged LIVE page running inside the notebook
- Hard rule carried over: no purple anywhere

## Share copy (draft)
I built a free notebook where AI artifacts run between your handwritten pages. Paste, Save, it runs. Web, Android, Windows. No account, no ads.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed under narration
- Music: original score, electric piano + bass + brushed kit + glockenspiel (world) and a chiptune pulse (the sprite)
- Treatment: 100 BPM; drops to a pad under the two promise freezes (sandbox, encryption); low-passed at night
- SFX posture: moderate, motion-matched (clicks, keys, pen, tape, 8-bit sprite moves)
- Restraint rule: no effect on a word onset; nothing louder than the voice
