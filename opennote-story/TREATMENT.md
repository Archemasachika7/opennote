# "The One Coloured Thing" · OpenNote founder story · Treatment

Style: **Urban Sketch · Pen & Wash**. 1920×1080, 24 fps, ~150 s. English narrator (Kokoro), handwritten captions in the page margin + `.srt`. No storyboard stop.
Engine: Lemo-Opuscar (the style's pen-and-wash engine). `/brag` sets the angle, hook and share copy; HyperFrames' storyline method (three different films, pick one) shapes section 1.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **A diary of nights.** Six sketchbook spreads, one per night ("night 1 … night 40"), each turned by his hand; the problem on the first pages, the build in the middle, the app on the last. Honest, but even and episodic: every page has the same weight and the reveal has nowhere to rise from.
2. **Before / after on one desk.** The same desk drawn twice, the old app on the left page, OpenNote on the right. Clear, but it turns a person's story into a comparison chart, and it needs the rival app on screen, which we can't show.
3. **The one coloured thing.** The student's world is drawn in ink only. The only colour in the film is the visualiser the AI makes for him: an amber-and-mint wave. When he screenshots it into his notes, the colour drains to grey ink. When he and Claude finally make it run inside a notebook, colour arrives and floods the entire sketchbook. The colour *is* the story.

**Chosen: 3.** It uses the style's two native moves at the emotional turns (the one coloured thing; colour as arrival), it needs no rival brand on screen, and it makes the abstract pain ("I can't keep the artifact") visible as loss of colour.

## 2. Logline and arc

A student who can't afford, upload, or keep what helps him learn asks an AI to build the notebook he needs, and the colour comes back; the student was Archishman all along.

- **Setup (0–50 s)** ink only. Paywall on a new notebook; uploads are premium; then the AI's visualiser, the first colour in the film. A screenshot drains it.
- **Turn (50–80 s)** he asks a different question. Night after night of building, pages turned.
- **Rise (80–122 s)** the first live page runs; colour arrives across the whole page; OpenNote's features drawn in colour.
- **Reveal and end (122–150 s)** "That student is Archishman." Free, no ads, no account, web / Android / Windows, the code on GitHub. The sketchbook closes.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Paperman (2012)**: learn the monochrome world with one coloured object, and colour as emotion. Don't take characters, the plane, any shot.
- **Urban-sketching reportage pages**: learn margin handwriting, date/time corner notes, line before wash. Don't take any artist's drawings.

## 4. Rights and facts (red lines)

- The paid notebook app is **never named or drawn**: a generic unnamed app with its own invented paywall copy.
- Claude is credited **in words** (voice and margin handwriting). The AI chat is drawn as a generic sketched chat window, no Anthropic logo or UI.
- OpenNote's screens use the real copy ("New live page", "Save", "LIVE", "Tap ink to seek", "Generate flashcards from Tape", "Unlock sync").
- **"Open source" is not said** while the repo has no LICENSE file; the film says "the code is on GitHub". One VO line (v15) is written to be swapped if a licence is added.
- Archishman is the creator and agreed to be named.
