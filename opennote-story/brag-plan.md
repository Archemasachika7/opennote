# Brag Plan: OpenNote, the founder story

## What is this app?
A free, offline, no-account notebook where AI-made visualisers run between handwritten pages; built by a student, Archishman, together with Claude.

## The angle
"The one coloured thing." The student's world is ink only; the only colour is the visualiser Claude made him. A screenshot drains it. When OpenNote runs it, colour floods the sketchbook.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A blank sketchbook page, the handwritten note "11:40 pm · two days before the exam", and the pen starting to draw a student at a desk.

## Key moments
- The unnamed app's paywall card ("Notebook limit reached.") and "Importing PDFs is a Premium feature."
- The visualiser blooms into colour, then drains to grey as a screenshot. One bar of silence.
- Night pages: "the ink lags", "pages turn black · fixed", "paste → Save → ?"
- The first live page runs and the colour arrives across the whole spread.

## Outro / punchline
"That student is Archishman. He asked Claude, and together they built OpenNote." The sketchbook closes: "notes that run."

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic (sincere, not loud)
- Creative direction: a reportage sketchbook, a jazz waltz that grows from one guitar to a band
- Interpretation: slow, honest pacing; the spectacle is the line drawing itself and colour as emotion

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080 · 24 fps
## Duration: 150 s (the user asked for ~150 s; overrides brag's 15–25 s default)

## Audio direction
Jazz waltz 3/4 at 112 BPM: nylon guitar alone → + vibraphone on the bloom → a bar of silence → + walking bass and brushes → full band with clarinet lead in F major when colour arrives → final chord as the book closes.
